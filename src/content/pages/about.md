I'm Cory Pleasance, a third-year Data Science & AI student at the University of Stirling. I'm British and Maltese, and I moved around a fair bit before university, living in India, Canterbury and London before coming up to Scotland.

Most of my time goes into LLM security: finding the ways language models, and the agents built on them, can be pushed into doing things they shouldn't, and building systems that find those failures automatically. Outside research I enter machine-learning competitions and sometimes build things close to the metal.
